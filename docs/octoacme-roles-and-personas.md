# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers oversee the health, growth, and delivery capacity of their engineering teams. They bridge the gap between technical execution and organizational goals, ensuring that teams are staffed, supported, and unblocked.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team staffing, hiring, and capacity planning
- Coach and mentor developers on technical and career growth
- Support delivery by removing blockers and handling escalations
- Collaborate with Project Managers on timelines, risks, and resource constraints
- Align with Product Managers on engineering priorities and trade-offs

### Goals
- Build high-performing, sustainable engineering teams
- Ensure team health and psychological safety
- Enable consistent delivery against commitments

### Typical Communication
- 1:1s with direct reports
- Engineering leadership syncs and cross-team coordination calls
- Escalation pathways and capacity reviews with Project Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: collaborates on timelines, resource constraints, and risk mitigation
- **Developers**: provides coaching, removes blockers, supports career development
- **Product Managers**: aligns engineering capacity and priorities with product roadmap

---

## Technical Lead / Solution Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Solution Architects provide technical direction and ensure the overall system design supports product goals. They make or guide architectural decisions and help translate product requirements into implementable technical approaches.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and system architecture
- Lead design reviews and establish technical standards
- Identify technical dependencies and constraints early in planning
- Evaluate build vs. buy decisions and integration approaches
- Support developers in complex problem-solving and implementation guidance

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure systems
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risk
- Ensure technical feasibility of product commitments

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and design documents
- Design review sessions with developers and product stakeholders
- Technical briefings for Project Managers on constraints and trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: translates product goals into technical designs and supports implementation
- **Project Managers**: surfaces technical risks and constraints that impact timelines
- **Product Managers**: validates feasibility and informs scope decisions based on technical realities

---

## QA / Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA and Test Engineers define and execute test strategies to ensure software meets quality standards and acceptance criteria before release. They advocate for quality throughout the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategies, plans, and coverage requirements
- Develop and maintain automated and manual test suites
- Validate acceptance criteria and regression coverage
- Assess release readiness and communicate quality risks
- Collaborate with developers on testability and defect resolution

### Goals
- Ensure high product quality and reliability
- Reduce defect escape rate to production
- Enable confident, low-risk releases

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality reports shared with the team
- Bug reports and regression summaries
- Release readiness sign-offs for Project Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: collaborates on quality practices, testability, and defect resolution
- **Project Managers**: provides release readiness feedback and flags quality risks that may affect timelines
- **Product Managers**: validates that delivered features meet acceptance criteria and user expectations

---

## Delivery Manager / Program Manager

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers and Program Managers coordinate across multiple projects or workstreams to ensure alignment, manage dependencies, and drive milestone governance for larger initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate cross-team delivery activities and dependencies
- Govern milestones, program-level risks, and escalations
- Maintain visibility into multiple simultaneous workstreams
- Facilitate alignment meetings across project teams
- Support escalation paths and stakeholder communications at the program level

### Goals
- Ensure cross-team alignment and delivery coherence
- Minimize inter-team blockers and dependency delays
- Maintain program-level transparency for senior stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Program status reports and milestone dashboards
- Cross-team dependency tracking and escalation logs
- Stakeholder briefings and executive summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: complements individual project coordination for larger initiatives spanning multiple teams
- **Product Managers**: ensures roadmap alignment across multiple product workstreams
- **Stakeholders**: provides consolidated program-level reporting and escalation support

---

## Executive Sponsor / Business Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors and Business Stakeholders provide strategic direction, funding decisions, and approval authority for projects. They ensure initiatives align with organizational priorities and validate that outcomes meet business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic alignment and business context for project goals
- Approve key decisions, scope changes, and escalations
- Validate that delivered outcomes meet business success criteria
- Advocate for project resources and organizational support
- Participate in milestone reviews and final acceptance

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between project outcomes and strategic objectives
- Enable informed decision-making at the organizational level

### Typical Communication
- Executive briefings and milestone reviews
- Decision escalation channels with Project and Product Managers
- Periodic status updates and final sign-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: receives status updates, provides approvals, and acts as escalation point
- **Product Managers**: aligns on product vision, business priorities, and success metrics
- **Delivery Managers**: receives program-level reporting and provides strategic direction

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX and Design Leads are responsible for the user experience quality of products. They translate user needs and business goals into interaction designs, workflows, and visual specifications that guide development.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows, interaction patterns, and design specifications
- Conduct or coordinate user research and usability testing
- Collaborate with Product Managers to align design with user needs and priorities
- Work with Developers on implementation feasibility and design fidelity
- Maintain and evolve design system components

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce rework caused by usability issues discovered late
- Ensure design consistency across product surfaces

### Typical Communication
- Design specs, prototypes, and user flow diagrams
- Design review sessions with developers and PMs
- Usability test results and design iteration notes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: collaborates on user needs, feature definitions, and acceptance criteria
- **Developers**: provides design specs and implementation guidance; validates design fidelity in delivered features
- **QA / Test Engineers**: aligns on usability and acceptance criteria validation

---

## Operations / Release Coordinator

### Role Summary
Operations Engineers and Release Coordinators manage deployment logistics, environment readiness, and operational communication to ensure smooth and reliable releases.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment schedules and release logistics
- Verify environment readiness and deployment prerequisites
- Execute and validate operational runbooks and checklists
- Communicate release status to stakeholders and the wider team
- Support rollback plans and incident response coordination

### Goals
- Ensure reliable, low-risk deployments
- Minimize release-related incidents and downtime
- Maintain operational transparency throughout the release lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and deployment runbooks
- Go/no-go status updates to Project Managers and stakeholders
- Post-deployment reports and incident summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: coordinates deployment activities and validates release artifacts
- **QA / Test Engineers**: aligns on release readiness and final validation sign-off
- **Project Managers**: provides deployment status and flags operational risks that may affect release timelines
